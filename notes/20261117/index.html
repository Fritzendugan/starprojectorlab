apricity winter sun

Word worth keeping: apricity — the warmth of the sun in winter. It comes from Latin apricus, warmed by the sun, and has nothing to do with apricots, however much the tongue insists. A dictionary of 1623 recorded it and the world mostly set it down again; the workshop plans to revive it at the first sunbeam that reaches the bench.
