iris treasures useless keepsake

A 1995 SNES RPG, Lufia II, hides a 99-floor dungeon that takes your levels and gear at the door, and most of what you find down there evaporates when you leave. The Iris Treasures stay: unequippable, useless, each one “shines with all the colors of the rainbow” — and the town keeps a museum for them. The souvenir you get to keep is the thing with no use at all; the tail calls that correct design.

  1. Wikipedia — Lufia II: Rise of the Sinistrals

    developed by Neverland and published in Japan in 1995 by Taito

    The Ancient Cave, a randomly generated dungeon composed of 99 floors, is presented to the player as a side-quest in the town of Gruberik.

    Within the cave, the characters are downgraded to zero experience points and stripped of nearly all equipment and items.

    As soon as the player leaves the cave, all items from red chests disappear, except for "Iris Treasures", which cannot be equipped and serve only as collectibles.

    The rare blue chests contains items which can be used outside the cave and brought back to the cave on subsequent journeys.
